Istanbul is a city in Turkey that connects Europe and Asia. It is famous for its rich history, beautiful mosques, colorful markets, and Bosphorus views.

Top attractions in Istanbul include Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque, Topkapi Palace, Galata Tower, Grand Bazaar, and Dolmabahce Palace.

For a 3-day trip, visitors can spend the first day exploring Sultanahmet, including Hagia Sophia and Blue Mosque. The second day can include a Bosphorus cruise, Galata Tower, and Istiklal Street. The third day can include shopping at Grand Bazaar and visiting Dolmabahce Palace.

The best areas to stay in Istanbul are Sultanahmet for history, Taksim for nightlife and shopping, and Karakoy for cafes and modern vibes.

Public transportation includes metro, tram, ferry, and buses. The Istanbulkart is useful for moving around the city.

A moderate daily budget for Istanbul can range between 60 and 120 USD depending on accommodation, food, shopping, and activities.
